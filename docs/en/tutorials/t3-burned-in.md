# T3 — Clean burned-in labels

## Goal
Detect and remove burned-in text on one series while keeping L/R markers.

## Steps
1. Ensure OCR models are ready in AI Features.
2. Open Series View → Detect Text.
3. Click green rectangles to whitelist keepers → Remove Text → Save.
