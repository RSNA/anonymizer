# Synthetic PHI pools (en_US)

Hand-curated common US given names, surnames, street stems, and city/state/ZIP triples
for hide-in-plain-sight replacement. Inspired by the same family of public SSA decade
baby-name tops and US Census surname frequency lists used by industry tools (e.g. Faker).

These are **not** a dump of any live person registry. Tokens are common-looking and
combinatorial; they do not identify real individuals.

Extract / curation date: 2026-10-05.
